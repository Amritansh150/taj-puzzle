For The CLAD Summer Hackathon Project Title: Taj Puzzle: Architectural Heritage Learning Game

Project Overview
Taj Puzzle is an interactive, spatial AR learning game designed to make discovering Moghul architecture engaging, tactile, and intuitive. Built using Lens Studio and optimized for Snap Spectacles hand tracking, the experience bridges spatial computing with architectural education. Players actively construct the Taj Mahal and its surrounding complex in their physical environment, learning structural geometry, symmetry, and historical layout through direct 1:1 spatial interaction.

By transforming architectural study into an immersive hands-free puzzle, Taj Puzzle replaces static textbook diagrams with tactile, hands-on assembly—helping learners understand how individual structural elements fit into a world-famous monument.

Key Features & Mechanics

Direct Spatial Tabletop Placement: Players anchor the interactive puzzle onto any physical table or desk using World Tracking surface detection, turning their personal space into a digital architectural workshop.

Two-Stage Progressive Learning Levels:

Level 1 (Mausoleum Construction): Focuses on structural architectural components. Players pick up individual pieces—including the iconic bulbous dome, marble minarets, central iwans (arches), and the raised plinth base—using natural pinch gestures to assemble the main mausoleum.

Level 2 (Complex Master Plan): Expands to site layout and spatial planning. Players arrange full, distinct structures—the central Mausoleum, the Western Mosque, the Eastern Jawab (Guest House), and the Southern Darwaza (Main Gateway)—to learn the symmetrical master plan of the entire complex.

Proximity Snapping & Precision Feedback: Dynamic spatial distance checks give players immediate feedback. When a piece is moved near its correct historical location, it automatically snaps into place, locking into position with visual highlights and confirming successful assembly.

Hands-Free Spatial Interaction: Built ground-up for hands-free AR glasses, allowing players to reach out, grab, rotate, and place 3D architectural pieces naturally using raw hand gestures.

Theme Alignment: Create

Faster: Removes complex UI overlays and tedious controls, letting learners assemble and experiment with 3D architectural models in seconds through direct spatial manipulation.

Easier: Demystifies intricate architectural concepts—such as Islamic bilateral symmetry, proportions, and complex layout—by breaking them down into digestible, interactive 3D building blocks.

More Intuitively: Leverages physical depth perception, spatial memory, and natural hand movement to make learning about cultural heritage feel as effortless and engaging as playing with physical building blocks.

Target Hardware & Tech Stack

Platform: Snap Spectacles / Lens Studio

Core Systems: World Tracking, Hand Tracking (Pinch & Drag Interactions), Proximity Snap Logic, Dynamic Level Manager

Content Pipeline: Sliced and modularized 3D architectural assets optimized for mobile spatial rendering
